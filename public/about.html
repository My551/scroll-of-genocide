Scroll of Genocide

A small tribute to the scroll of genocide from NetHack. Name a class of creatures, and the gods decide whether they are wiped from the world.

Only real, living creatures can be wiped out. Creatures of myth and fiction no longer exist in this world, and the gods refuse anything aimed at people.

What is saved

Every answer is saved anonymously to build the Tally of the Slain that all readers can see. The game stores only the words you wrote, the verdict and the time.

No accounts, names, email addresses or IP addresses are stored. To stop flooding, the server keeps a scrambled code that changes every day and cannot be traced back to you.

Answers are judged by an AI model running on Cloudflare. Refused answers are never shown publicly.
